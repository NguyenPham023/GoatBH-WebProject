Our Story

EduTech Solutions began when a small group of students and developers noticed the same problem in online classes: the tools delivered content and did little to keep learners engaged, and many were hard to use with assistive technology.

We set out to build learning tools that are accessible first and smart second, so lessons adapt to the learner rather than the other way round.

We started with a few lightweight classroom tools and listened closely to teachers and students. Their feedback still helps shape every release.

Why This Role Matters

As a Front-End Developer, you will build the interfaces behind our adaptive learning platform, shaping how thousands of students learn math and science every day. Your work will make our tools faster, clearer and accessible to learners on any device.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain responsive React web applications for our learning platform.
  • Work with UI/UX and instructional designers to create interactive learning modules.
  • Optimise performance for mobile devices and low-bandwidth connections.
  • Make sure every feature meets accessibility standards (WCAG).
  • Take part in code reviews, sprint planning and agile ceremonies.
  • Test features with real teachers and students and act on their feedback.

Requirements and Qualifications

Essential

  • 3+ years of experience in front-end development (React, HTML5, CSS3, REST APIs or GraphQL).
  • Working knowledge of accessibility and responsive design.
  • Comfortable with Git and collaborative workflows.
  • A strong interest in education technology and user-centred design.

Preferable

  • Experience in EdTech or learning management system (LMS) development.
  • Familiarity with integrating AI features, such as recommendation tools.
  • Exposure to cloud deployment (AWS or GCP).

Compensation and Benefits

Salary: $1,200 – $2,500 USD per month, depending on experience.

  • Learning and conference budget
  • Flexible working hours
  • Health insurance
  • Annual team retreat

Full-Time

Salary

Chief Technology Officer

Why This Role Matters

Inclusion is one of our core values, and this role makes it real. You will catch barriers before learners meet them, so that every student, including those with disabilities, can use our tools with confidence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Test new features against WCAG 2.2 using keyboards and screen readers.
  • Run automated accessibility checks and validate HTML and CSS.
  • Write clear bug reports with steps to reproduce and suggested fixes.
  • Work with developers and designers to fix issues before release.
  • Keep an accessibility checklist for the whole team.

Requirements and Qualifications

Essential

  • Understanding of semantic HTML, ARIA basics and colour contrast.
  • Experience testing websites on different browsers and devices.
  • Attention to detail and clear written communication.

Preferable

  • Experience with screen readers such as NVDA or VoiceOver.
  • Familiarity with Jira or similar issue trackers.
  • An accessibility certification or relevant coursework.

Compensation and Benefits

Salary: $900 – $1,800 USD per month, depending on experience.

  • Learning and conference budget
  • Flexible working hours
  • Health insurance
  • Annual team retreat
